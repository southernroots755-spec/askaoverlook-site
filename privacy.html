Privacy

Last updated October 2026.

This site is run by SRH 1 LLC, which operates Aska Overlook. The pages themselves don't use advertising trackers, and we don't sell your information.

When you check dates or book, the booking calendar and checkout are provided by Hospitable, our booking system. Hospitable collects the details needed to make your reservation, such as your name, contact details, payment card and ID check, and processes your payment. Its privacy policy explains how it handles that information.

We use your booking details to run your stay: to send your door code and check-in information, answer your questions, and meet legal requirements such as lodging tax records.

Questions about your information? Call or text (706) 514-6297, or email josh@southernrootsholding.com.
