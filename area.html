Things to do near Aska Overlook

The cabin is in Blue Ridge's Aska area, between Lake Blue Ridge and the Toccoa River. Downtown is about five minutes away once you're down the mountain. Here's what our family keeps going back to.

Trails and waterfalls

Aska Trails

A network of looping trails in the national forest, from about one mile to five and a half, popular with hikers and mountain bikers alike.

Fall Branch Falls

A short walk on the Benton MacKaye Trail to a double waterfall, lined with mountain laurel and rhododendron. Plan on about half an hour round trip.

Swinging Bridge

A long suspension footbridge over the Toccoa River, reached on the Benton MacKaye Trail. Good for a picnic and a look down at the river.

Long Creek Falls and Springer Mountain

Further out, a walk of about two miles round trip from Three Forks follows the Appalachian Trail to a two-tier waterfall. Springer Mountain, the trail's southern end, is in Fannin County too.

On the water

Lake Blue Ridge

Clear mountain water a little closer than downtown. The marina rents boats, paddleboards and paddle boats.

Toccoa River

Outfitters run tubing, kayaking and rafting trips in the warmer months, and guides lead trout-fishing trips on the river.

In and around town

Downtown Blue Ridge

A walkable historic downtown with shops, galleries, restaurants and breweries.

Blue Ridge Scenic Railway

A vintage train ride from downtown along the Toccoa River to the Tennessee line and back, with a layover for lunch.

Mercier Orchards

A family orchard with dozens of apple varieties, cider, baked goods and seasonal you-pick.

Wineries and adventure parks

Mountain-view vineyards, zipline courses and trail rides are all a short drive away.

Hours, seasons and trail access change, so check before you go. For more ideas, see Explore Georgia's guide to Blue Ridge.
